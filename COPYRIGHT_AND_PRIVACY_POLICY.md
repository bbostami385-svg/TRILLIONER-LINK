# TRILLIONER LINK — Copyright and Privacy Protection Policy

> This is a platform policy, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Before public launch, the owner should have local counsel review the notice-and-takedown, counter-notice, retention, and child-safety language for every country where the service is offered.

## 1. Original work and ownership

- The uploader keeps ownership of original video, audio, images, writing, and other work they create.
- Uploading grants TRILLIONER LINK only the limited license needed to host, transcode, stream, moderate, secure, and display the work according to the user's settings.
- Users may not download, screen-capture, screen-record, edit, re-upload, or commercially redistribute another person's work without permission or a lawful exception.
- Creator protection defaults are **downloads disabled**, **visible watermark enabled**, and **re-upload/report review enabled**.

## 2. Copyright report process

A claimant can report an unauthorized copy from the protected video viewer. Copyright claims require:

1. Contact email;
2. A detailed description of the original work and alleged infringement;
3. Optional original-work and evidence links;
4. A good-faith ownership or authorized-agent attestation.

Abusive, knowingly false, or fraudulent reports may be rejected and may affect reporting access. Every report receives a status: pending, under review, actioned, or rejected. Confirmed infringement can make the target video unavailable while the decision is recorded for audit and appeal.

## 3. Privacy and capture reports

Users can report screenshots, screen recordings, or video-call captures involving private content. Private Family Meetings use invite-only authorization, a privacy shield when the page is hidden, context-menu/copy deterrence, and clear participant warnings. A report does not expose private evidence to the accused user.

## 4. Review and appeal

Moderators should review ownership evidence, uploader history, timestamps, and both parties' explanations. Enforcement should be proportionate. The affected uploader must receive a reason and an appeal path. Emergency child-safety or intimate-image reports may use priority escalation and restricted evidence access.

## 5. Technical limitations

Web browsers and ordinary Android devices do not provide a reliable universal API to prevent a determined person from photographing a screen, using OS-level screen recording, or recording with another device. TRILLIONER LINK therefore uses defense in depth:

- protected playback and download policy;
- visible and, where appropriate, user/session watermarks;
- context-menu and casual-copy deterrence;
- privacy shield on hidden meeting pages;
- access control, audit logs, reports, takedowns, and account enforcement;
- native Android builds may additionally use platform secure-window flags where available.

The product must never promise that screenshots or recordings are impossible.

## 6. Evidence and retention

Collect the minimum evidence needed for review, restrict staff access, encrypt stored evidence, record reviewer actions, and delete evidence according to the approved retention schedule. Do not request passwords, identity documents, or unnecessary private call recordings in a report.
